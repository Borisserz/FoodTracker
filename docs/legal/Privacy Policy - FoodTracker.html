Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FoodTracker (“the App”, “we”, “us”), developed by Boris Serzhanovich, handles your information. The App is a nutrition, diet, and calorie tracking application for iOS. By using the App you agree to the practices described below.

2. HealthKit Data

When you grant permission, the App reads health metrics (such as active energy burned, workouts, and body weight) from Apple HealthKit solely to display them inside the App and compute nutrition insights. We also write data (such as dietary energy, water intake, logged activities, and weight) to HealthKit if you explicitly allow it. We do not use HealthKit data for advertising, data brokering, or marketing, and we do not share HealthKit data with third parties. HealthKit data is processed on your device.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

Your personal nutrition records — meals, custom recipes, logs, weight history, and settings — are stored on your device using SwiftData. Depending on the feature, data is stored as follows:

  • iCloud sync (Apple CloudKit) — If you are signed in to iCloud, your nutrition data (meals, custom recipes, daily summaries, and weight history) is synced across your devices using your private iCloud account (CloudKit private database, container iCloud.com.borisdev.FoodTracker2026). This data is stored in your personal iCloud space, is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, and is not accessible to us.
  • Before & After Progress Photos — Stored locally on your device inside Application Support with iOS file protection and excluded from iCloud backups. They are never uploaded unless you explicitly consent and tap AI analysis.
  • Cloud Content & Quotas — The App fetches curated Academy articles, recipes, and diet plans from Google Firebase Firestore (read-only), and stores weekly API rate-limit counters linked to your Firebase UID and installation identifier.
  • Ecosystem Sync (WorkoutTracker) — If you also install our companion app WorkoutTracker, daily workout calories and pure water totals can be exchanged locally on-device via the shared App Group (group.com.borisdev.WorkoutTracker).

4. AI Features & Third-Party Processing Consent

FoodTracker asks for your explicit in-app consent before sending photos or personal nutrition/mood/chat context to third-party AI services. You can grant or revoke this consent anytime in Settings → AI Data & Photo Privacy.

When you consent and invoke an AI feature (Plate Scan, Menu Helper, Fridge-to-Recipe, AI Coach Chat, AI Chef, 7-Day Plan Builder, Mood Advice, or Before/After Analysis), your request is sent over encrypted HTTPS to our backend proxy (Firebase Cloud Functions), authenticated via Firebase Auth and Firebase App Check, and forwarded to OpenRouter and downstream third-party AI model providers. We configure OpenRouter requests with data_collection: "deny" so images and prompts are processed transiently and not retained for model training. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal identifiers in free-text prompts.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for tracking across apps or websites owned by other companies. Third-party processors listed above receive only the minimum data needed to execute the requested search or AI analysis.

7. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all app data at any time inside the App: open the AI Coach tab (or tap your profile avatar on Home) → Profile & Settings → Settings (gear icon) → Account → Delete account.

This action deletes your Firebase Authentication account (including revoking Sign in with Apple tokens if used), wipes server-side usage/quota records and shared ecosystem data associated with your UID, and permanently deletes all local SwiftData records, cached items, AI consent state, and local progress photos. Note that private iCloud CloudKit records belong to your personal Apple ID and can be managed in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud. This action cannot be undone.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
